Support

How the game works, what to do when a purchase or a save is not where you expect it, and how to reach a person who will actually reply.

Effective: November 12, 2026

Email support@dahvio.com and a real person answers — there is only one of us. Say whether you are on iPhone or Android and what you were doing when something went wrong, and it will get sorted a lot faster.

How the game works

Mile Marker is a road trip you read. Pick a road and a traveler, then choose what to do at each stop. Four numbers decide how far you get: Tank (fuel), Wallet (cash), Nerve and Gut. Run the tank dry and you are stranded; get stranded twice and the run is over.

A yellow choice has a roll in it: the game checks your Nerve or Gut against how hard the thing is, and it can go either way. A blue choice costs money. A green choice is the predictable one. A white choice marked closed tells you what you would have needed.

Losing is part of it. A new run on the same road keeps what you learned about where the pumps are, and nothing else.

The four roads

Route 1, the Southwest Desert Run, is free as far as Kingman at mile 300. Routes 2 and 3, the Deep South and the Pacific Coast, can be bought and driven in any order. Route 4, the Hi-Line, opens once you have arrived at the end of two other roads, because it remembers what you brought back from them.

Purchases

Each road is a one-time purchase, and all four are available together for less. There is no subscription and nothing in the game sells a way past losing.

Purchases are billed by Apple or Google and are tied to your store account, not to the phone. On a new device, or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the first screen. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through your Play order history.

Saved progress

The game saves at every stop. Close it whenever you like and the first screen offers to continue. A run that ends, by arriving or by being stranded for good, is not resumable; that is deliberate.

Progress is stored on the device and travels with your normal device backup. It is not synced between an iPhone and an Android phone.

Something is wrong

If a road you paid for shows a price, tap Restore purchases while online. If the price shows but the purchase sheet never appears, check that purchases are allowed on the device (Screen Time on iPhone, parental controls on Android).

If the text stops appearing mid-scene, close and reopen the app; it resumes at the last stop. If it happens twice in the same place, email the name of the stop shown on the green sign at the top and we will fix it.

Typos and wrong mileages

Genuinely welcome. The roads are real and the distances are measured, so if a sign is wrong, say which one.
