Privacy Policy

Mile Marker is a game you read. It keeps your place on the road on your own phone, and this explains the one place anything leaves it.

Effective: November 12, 2026

The short version: your saved game stays on your device. There is no account, no server holding your progress, no analytics and no ads. The only thing that leaves your phone is what the App Store or Google Play needs in order to sell you a route.

1. Who We Are

Mile Marker is developed and published by DahVio Studios, a solo developer studio based in Long Beach, California, operated by Ray (support@dahvio.com).

2. What Is Stored On Your Device

The game writes a small amount of data to your device so that it can pick up where you left off:

  • The run in progress: which road, which traveler, the name you typed for them, your tank, wallet, Nerve and Gut, and the choices that run has made
  • Your record on each road: how many runs, how many arrivals, which endings you have seen
  • The thing you kept from each road you finished, which later roads remember
  • Which roads you have bought, so the game opens without asking the store every time

None of this is sent to DahVio Studios. There is no account and no sign-in, because there is nothing on our end to sign in to. The name you give your traveler never leaves the device.

3. Purchases

Routes are sold by Apple (on iPhone) or Google (on Android) as one-time purchases. Payment is handled entirely by them; we never see your card, your name or your email.

The app uses RevenueCat to ask the store what you own and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your installation, the purchases made by that installation, and basic technical details the store attaches to a purchase such as country and app version. It does not receive anything you have done in the game. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Your Device Backup

Saved progress lives in the app's own storage, so it is included in your device's normal backup if you use iCloud Backup or Google's device backup. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. DahVio Studios has no access to it.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses
  • Your location. The roads in the game are written; the app never asks where you are
  • Advertising identifiers. The game has no ads and no ad SDKs
  • In-game analytics or behavioural tracking of any kind
  • Contacts, photos, microphone or camera. The app requests no device permissions

6. The Web Version

A test build of the game also runs in a browser at dahvio.com. It keeps the same saved-game data in that browser's local storage and nowhere else. The web page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives that request the way it would for any website that uses them. The app bundles its typefaces and makes no such request.

7. Deleting Your Data

Deleting the app removes its saved data from your device permanently. Nothing is held anywhere else for us to delete on your behalf. Purchases stay attached to your Apple or Google account, and Restore Purchases on the first screen brings them back after a reinstall.

8. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it and the revised version is posted here. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else: support@dahvio.com
